## Fields populated by batch_gate.py

- `{batch_id}` — sequential per Wave
- `{wave}` — current Wave (0a, 0b, 1a, ...)
- `{n_done}`, `{n_total}` — story counts
- `{hours}`, `{minutes}` — sum of `claude -p` wall-time
- Cost table rows — sum from `~/.claude/logs/` per stage
- Review notes — extracted from `_bmad/auto-dev-state/checkpoint-log/`
